# Methods for Equine Light Therapy Evidence Map

Search PubMed and Europe PMC, then screen titles and abstracts for relevant original work in live horses. Exclude unrelated uses of light, work on other species, and cell or cadaver studies. Link companion publications that cover the same experiment so they count once. Flag the five live-horse studies added from the 2023 Millis and Bergh review separately. Group the eligible rows by tissue and light modality.

## Interpretation

The 30 database-search studies include one linked pair of reports counted as one experiment. The expanded view adds five studies sourced through a review. The totals in the matrix correspond to those respective scopes. A study result applies to the device, site, sample, and outcome described in its source.

## Collection and limits

Build the comparison group from the rival LED products. The 40-product comparison group represents the selected LED listings. Product pages provide published specifications rather than a controlled measurement series. Changes in the listing or sale terms after collection can alter the comparison. The supplied SVGs are from November 24 and summarize the earlier 37-product disclosure group and 21 eligible single-unit prices.

See the [method note](METHOD.md) for the collection rules and the [dataset page](https://nickerhorse.com/pages/horse-red-light-device-spec-index-2026) for the accompanying analysis.

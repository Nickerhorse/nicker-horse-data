## Collection and limits

Combine statistics abstracted from PubMed records with estimates transcribed from USDA APHIS NAHMS reports. The records combine different populations and outcomes, so compare rows only after checking their denominators. The reported breakdown applies to lame equids in the USDA survey, and the review did not locate a direct estimate covering all equids in a US national source. S062 is a derived estimate for abscess- or puncture-related lameness within the survey frame.

See the [method note](METHOD.md) for the collection rules and the [dataset page](https://nickerhorse.com/pages/horse-hoof-and-leg-health-statistics) for the accompanying analysis.

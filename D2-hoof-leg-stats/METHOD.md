# Methods for Hoof & Leg Problem Stats

Combine statistics abstracted from PubMed records with estimates transcribed from USDA APHIS NAHMS reports. Keep the original sample and time frame attached to each row. For S062, multiply the survey's 12-month lameness percentage by the percentage among lame equids attributed to abscess or puncture, then divide by 100. Record the resulting approximation separately from the source estimates.

## Interpretation

Report the population, period, and case definition alongside a value. For S005, the audit found that the USDA source reports an abscess-or-puncture share within lame equids. The reviewed reports and research abstracts did not yield a direct national rate of hoof abscess among all equids. For S062 the audit approximates a 95% interval of 2.0 to 3.5 per 100 equids in the USDA survey frame, with covariance omitted.

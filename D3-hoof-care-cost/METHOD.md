# Methods for US Hoof Care Cost

Keep each observation with its service definition, billing unit, and scope. Derive annual routine-care scenarios from a per-visit amount multiplied by 52 divided by the assumed interval in weeks. The later clinic observations are a convenience sample of four fee lists checked November 25, 2026. Combine fee components only within the same clinic, excluding amounts outside the published schedule.

## Interpretation

Retain the per-visit or per-year unit when using a figure. Some published fees depend on distance or service category. The image scenario uses a two-to-four-view assumption and only source-listed charges. The recurring-care inputs in the JSON apply to farrier scheduling, excluding case-specific expenses.
